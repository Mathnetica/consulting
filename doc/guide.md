Build a premium consulting website for **Mathnetica** using the latest stable **Next.js App Router**, **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **shadcn/ui**.

The site should feel inspired by the minimalism, typography, pacing, whitespace, and premium product-company feel of poetic.com, but it must NOT copy Poetic’s layout, text, branding, visuals, or components.

The business goal is important:

Mathnetica is not primarily a research lab anymore.

It is a commercial Dutch engineering consultancy focused on:

1. Data & AI Platform Engineering
2. AI Architecture
3. Complex Systems Engineering
4. Architecture Reviews

Research remains an important part of the company, but it should support credibility and technical depth rather than dominate the homepage.

BRAND POSITIONING

Mathnetica should feel like a small, highly technical Dutch engineering boutique.

The company is built around senior engineers and architects who have:

* many years of hands-on software engineering experience
* experience designing and building complex production systems
* architecture expertise
* cloud/platform engineering experience
* Data & AI engineering experience
* enterprise architecture credentials such as TOGAF where relevant
* Kubernetes/cloud certifications where relevant
* the ability to both design architecture and understand implementation details

Core idea:

“Architecture by engineers.”

Alternative supporting lines:

“Senior enough to architect. Technical enough to build.”

“We design systems we know how to build.”

“Architecture without engineering is just a diagram.”

Do not make the brand sound like a generic AI consultancy.

Avoid phrases such as:

* unlock the power of AI
* digital transformation
* cutting-edge solutions
* innovative solutions
* AI-powered future
* end-to-end digital transformation

The tone should be intelligent, calm, technical, confident, understated, European, premium, and engineering-first.

VISUAL DIRECTION

Design inspiration:

* poetic.com
* modern Swiss editorial typography
* high-end architecture studios
* technical research institutions
* premium engineering consultancies

Visual principles:

* extreme simplicity
* very generous whitespace
* strong typography
* large display headlines
* restrained navigation
* subtle motion only
* no gradients unless nearly invisible
* no glossy AI graphics
* no neon blue/purple AI aesthetic
* no stock photography
* no generic laptop mockups
* no giant dashboard screenshots
* no excessive cards
* no visual clutter

Use an off-white or very light neutral background.

Use near-black text.

Optionally use one subtle accent color, but keep the interface almost monochrome.

Typography should feel modern and editorial.

Prefer a grotesk / neo-grotesk sans-serif look.

Use a high-quality open-source font through next/font/google or next/font/local if appropriate.

Good font directions:

* Geist
* Inter
* Manrope
* Instrument Sans
* IBM Plex Sans
* Space Grotesk

Choose one main font and use typography hierarchy rather than many fonts.

The design should feel expensive because of proportion, spacing, rhythm, and typography, not decoration.

TECH STACK

Use:

* latest stable Next.js
* App Router
* TypeScript
* Tailwind CSS
* shadcn/ui
* Lucide icons only where necessary
* Framer Motion only if genuinely useful
* next/font

Create reusable components.

Use server components by default.

Use client components only where interaction requires them.
